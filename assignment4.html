About His Majesty, The King

Welcome, welcome ladies and gentlemen! We are gathered here today to witness history. Please look at these royal portraits and feast your eyes upon King Charles the Fur-st! He doesn’t rule over the United Kingdom, but over the Guinea Kingdom. His reign is not made on politics, but on food like grass and apples. As you can see, he takes his royal duties very seriously - especially when it comes to his naptime. All hail King Charles! May he live long!
